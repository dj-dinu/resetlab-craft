04 / THE METHOD

Clear rules. Room for context.

These grades describe measured high-frequency coverage, across the whole track.

Bitrate ≠ grade
HQ

At least 30% around 20 kHz.

Measurable signal near 20 kHz must be present for at least 30% of the full decoded duration.

MQ / LQ

Then the 18 kHz rule.

If HQ is not met, at least 30% around 18 kHz earns MQ. Otherwise the spectral grade is LQ.

YOUR EARS MATTER

LQ does not mean a bad master.

Vintage recordings, filtered sounds, bass stems, and intentional lo-fi music can have limited bandwidth and sound exactly right.

A spectrum does not prove original bitrate, source authenticity, or audible quality. Noise can add high-frequency energy; silence still counts toward total duration.
