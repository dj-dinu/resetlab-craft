## Code reuse

You may run, study, modify and redistribute covered code, including commercially. When distributing covered source or binaries, preserve required copyright/license notices, provide the license and corresponding source under GPLv3, and mark modifications and their dates as required. Private modifications do not require public release. Providing source to recipients does not require contributing changes to this project's repository.

No extra restriction requires payment, forbids commercial forks or demands permission for GPL-permitted reuse. This summary explains the license; the GPL text governs. Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses and notices. Their binary/source/build/relinking requirements must be reviewed separately before distribution. A project license does not settle all bundled dependency obligations.

Ordinary findings reports and the user's music/metadata are not relicensed merely because the app processes them. No music, private settings, tokens or diagnostic caches belong in public source releases.

## Brand and origin

ResetLab Craft and Bandprism identify this project's official identity. This project does not grant rights under trademark law to use those identities as another product's branding, and it does not claim they are registered trademarks. Name/handle clearance and any registration remain separate release work.

- Accurately describe an unmodified redistribution, its original project and its distributor.
- Clearly identify modified builds as modified. Use a distinct product/distributor identity and do not imply that ResetLab Craft authored, approved or supports your fork.
- References such as “based on Bandprism by ResetLab Craft” may describe origin accurately. Preserve applicable author/license notices without presenting a fork as an official release.

Misrepresentation and trademark rights are separate from software-copying permission. Final ownership, dependency and brand terms still need release review; no exclusive trademark right is established by this policy alone.
